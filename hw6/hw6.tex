\section{Model Specification}

\subsection{Outcome and link function}

The outcome is pre-bronchodilator FFB status, \texttt{PreBD\_FFB}.
We define a binary outcome
\[
Y_{ij} = I(\texttt{PreBD\_FFB}_{ij} = \text{obstruction}),
\]
where $Y_{ij}=1$ indicates obstruction for subject $i$ at visit $j$.
Because the outcome is binary, we use a logit link and Bernoulli variance
function:
\[
\mathrm{logit}(\mu_{ij}) =
\log\left(\frac{\mu_{ij}}{1-\mu_{ij}}\right), \qquad
\mathrm{Var}(Y_{ij}) = \mu_{ij}(1-\mu_{ij}).
\]
Here $\mu_{ij}=P(Y_{ij}=1)$ is the marginal probability of obstruction.
This model gives population-averaged effects on the log odds of obstruction.

\subsection{Mean model}

The marginal mean model is
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{logit}(\mu_{ij})
= \beta_0 + TG_i + visit_j + TG_i:visit_j
+ age\_rz_i + gender_i + ethnic_i.
\end{equation}

Treatment group, visit, gender, and ethnicity are modeled as categorical
variables, and age at randomization is modeled as a continuous covariate.
The treatment-by-visit interaction allows the population-averaged trajectory
of obstruction to differ by treatment group.
Rather than listing all coefficients separately, the model contains an
intercept, treatment contrasts, visit indicators, treatment-by-visit
interactions, age, gender, and ethnicity contrasts.

Before fitting the model, time points with more than 75\% missingness in
\texttt{PreBD\_FFB} were removed. These were visits 44, 56, 64, and 120.
The observed logits before and after removing these visits are shown in
\cref{fig:prebd-ffb-logit-all,fig:prebd-ffb-logit-le75}.

The parameter estimates were broadly similar under the two working correlation
structures. The independence model had QIC 7674.83, while the AR(1) model had
QIC 7693.54. Since smaller QIC is preferred, the independence working
correlation was selected for the remaining inference.

\subsection{Interpretation}

Some sandwich standard errors differ materially from the model-based standard
errors, especially for baseline covariates such as age, gender, and ethnicity.
This suggests that the model-based variance may be sensitive to assumptions
about the working covariance. I use the sandwich standard errors for inference
because they are robust to misspecification of the working correlation in GEE.

\section{Hypothesis Testing and Interpretation}

\subsection{Hypothesis test}

To evaluate whether the population-averaged obstruction trajectory differs by
treatment group, we tested
\[
H_0:\ \text{all treatment-by-visit interaction coefficients are zero}
\]
against the alternative that at least one treatment-by-visit interaction is
nonzero. The Wald test result is shown in \cref{tab:parallel-test}.

The Wald statistic is 46.55 with 30 degrees of freedom, and the p-value is
0.028. Thus, we reject the null hypothesis at the 0.05 level and conclude that
the population-averaged obstruction trajectories differ by treatment group.

\subsection{Interpretation of effects}

For this binary outcome with a logit link, exponentiated coefficients would be
interpreted as population-averaged odds ratios. However, because the mean model
uses categorical visit indicators and many treatment-by-visit interactions, the
individual key parameter estimates are hard to interpret directly. The more
useful interpretation is the global evidence of different treatment trajectories
and the observed logit patterns in
\cref{fig:prebd-ffb-logit-all,fig:prebd-ffb-logit-le75}.

\section{Summary}

We fit a marginal logistic regression model using GEE for the probability of
pre-bronchodilator obstruction. The model included treatment group, visit,
treatment-by-visit interactions, age at randomization, gender, and ethnicity.
After comparing independence and AR(1) working correlation structures using QIC,
we selected the independence structure.

There is evidence that the population-averaged obstruction trajectory differs by
treatment group. The model is useful for estimating marginal treatment-group
patterns, but it has limitations. It does not provide subject-specific effects,
some visits were removed because of high missingness, and the categorical visit
interaction model has many parameters, which makes individual coefficient
interpretation difficult.
